About Me

My name is Francisco Gandiola. I am 27 years old and I was born and raised in Buenos Aires, Argentina. When I was 11, my family was referred to the missionaries, and I've got baptized six months later. I returned home from my mission in Lima about one year ago, and I've been trying to become self-reliant since then.
Nowadays, I am working as an in-field mentor for the MTC and developing computer and programming skills. Leaving work and studies aside, I enjoy singing, playing board games with friends and teaching.
